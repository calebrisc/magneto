# Orlando-Metro Public Fabrication Access (researched 2026-08-30)

Scope: makerspaces/fab labs within ~30 miles of Orlando usable by a non-student adult on a day pass or short membership, checked for resin/SLA, FDM, laser cutter, metal 3D printing, and silicone casting/vacuum chamber. Plus commercial rush services for titanium and resin.

## Headline finding

**No public-access Orlando-metro makerspace confirmed to have a resin/SLA printer** (Formlabs or otherwise) as of this research. Melrose Center and MakerFX explicitly list FDM-only fleets; FamiLAB's public pages/wiki show FDM printers (Flashforge, Bambu A1, Elegoo Neptune 4 FDM) with no resin printer mentioned anywhere searchable. Factur — the one Orlando space most likely to have had a resin printer — is **closed** (Yelp: closed as of April 2026, appears to have liquidated equipment). This should be confirmed by phone/visit before ruling any of them out, since equipment lists on org websites lag reality.

## Caveats

- Public-facing equipment lists (especially FamiLAB's and small makerspaces') can lag reality by months; a phone call or in-person visit is the only way to confirm a resin printer isn't quietly on-site somewhere.
- Factur's closure is based on a Yelp status flag (Apr 2026) — worth a direct call/visit check since it was previously the most likely local resin-printer venue.
- No day-pass fee could be confirmed for FamiLAB or MakerFX — both appear to be membership-first with no drop-in day rate advertised.
